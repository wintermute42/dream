Dream Dance

This page shows the dance of the stage that opened it. Open it from the panel: card “Movement” → Dream Dance → “Page ↗” (or key L on the stage).
The stage window is from before an update of Dream – this page needs the new one. Reload the stage (the picture pauses for a moment); this page comes back by itself.

Clutch

Released: your dance does nothing yet – engage the clutch to let it steer.
Engaged: your dance acts on top of what the sliders and the controllers set. Released: only they do – the dance fades out in a third of a second. Key K, a MIDI button (“Button: clutch in/out”) or the button in Dream Control.

Detection

The browser has not named a camera yet: allow the camera for Dream (it asks when the camera starts), then they are listed here.
Dream Dance has its own detection – what Flowtrack detects with (fire/LED too) stays Flowtrack’s. Only you change it: here or in the Movement card. The fingers’ switch is at the finger view below.

Movement set

A set is a named table of what your movements do. Choose one here, in the panel, with a MIDI button (“Button: next movement set”) or in a playlist scene.
